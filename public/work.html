How I build

From first message to handover

Listen

What does the business sell, and which language do its customers speak?

Design

Colours, type and one idea from the business’s own world, before any code.

Build

TypeScript or plain JavaScript, no heavy frameworks, fast on cheap phones.

Launch

Netlify hosting, forms and orders straight to the owner’s Telegram.

Hand over

A README so the owner knows where to change prices, texts and photos.
