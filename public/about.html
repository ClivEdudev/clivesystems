Hi, I’m Clive.

My name is Baxriddin Imonqulov. Online I go by Clive, just Clive. I teach English to Uzbek students, and I build the websites and apps that my students, my clients and I actually use.

My classroom runs on a site I wrote. When a lesson starts, it opens on every student’s phone and on the smart board. When I decided to learn German, I didn’t look for a course. I built Sprachwerk and started from the first sound.

For hotels, shops and cafés in Andijan I make sites that work on cheap phones and speak three languages. And when I need to focus, I grow a garden. That’s Vaqt, the newest planet here.

How I work

Five rules I keep

Phones first

My students and customers open everything on phones, often cheap ones.

Three languages

Uzbek, Russian and English, because that’s how people here talk.

No fake numbers

Prices, phones and reviews stay “to be confirmed” until the business confirms them.

Light code

Plain HTML, CSS and TypeScript. Fast to load, easy to hand over.

Old screens too

My class site runs on an old smart-board browser, so it works everywhere.
