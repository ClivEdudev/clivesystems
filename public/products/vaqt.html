Vaqt

Focus sessions grow an Uzbek garden: 34 plants, a bazaar, levels, daily quests and 57 awards. Works offline.

Inside

What it does

Grow a garden

34 Uzbek plants in five rarity tiers, from lola and rayhon to chinor, lotus and the eight-point star flower. Each one is drawn with real light and shade, and about one session in 25 grows a golden one.

The Bazaar

Spend focus coins on seeds, 16 decorations (from a tandir oven to a little minaret), six sceneries and streak freezes.

Levels and daily quests

Every focused minute is XP. Climb from Seed to Elder chinor, and pick up three fresh quests each day.

57 awards

Bronze to diamond, across growth, time, streaks, depth, habits and more. Each one pays out coins.

Deep focus

Strict: leave the tab and your plant wilts. Gentle: it only counts the interruptions.

Three ways to time

Timer with one-tap presets, Pomodoro cycles with short and long breaks, or Flow that counts up.

A living dial

The sky in the dial follows your time of day. Breaks bring a breathing circle and a stretch tip.

Tags and tasks

See where your hours go, pick a task, focus on it and tick it off.

Stats

The week by tag, a month calendar, your best hours, how focused you felt, and a 26-week heatmap.

Time tracker

Log time you spent away from the screen, edit any session, export to CSV.

Sounds and mini timer

Rain, brown noise, waves, fireplace, wind and a clock, made live in the browser, plus a floating timer window where supported.

Offline and private

Works without internet and installs like an app. Your data stays on your device unless you sync it.

How it works

Three steps

1

Choose a seed and a tag

Set the time on the dial, pick what you’re working on.

2

Press start, stay

Keep the tab open. Your plant grows in real time.

3

Watch the garden grow

Every session lands in your garden and your stats.

The seeds

34 plants from home

Lola and paxta are free. The other 32 unlock in the Bazaar with coins you earn by focusing, from common to legendary. Bigger trees want longer sessions.
